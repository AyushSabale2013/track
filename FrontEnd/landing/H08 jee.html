What is JEE?

The Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) is India's premier engineering entrance examination conducted for admission to the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs), National Institutes of Technology (NITs), IIITs, and other top engineering colleges.

JEE consists of two stages — JEE Main and JEE Advanced. It serves as the gateway for students aspiring to pursue engineering from India's most prestigious institutions.

The examination evaluates candidates in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, demanding strong conceptual understanding, analytical thinking, and problem-solving abilities.
