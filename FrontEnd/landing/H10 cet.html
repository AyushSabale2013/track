What is MHT-CET?

The Maharashtra Common Entrance Test (MHT-CET) is a state-level entrance examination conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra for admission to engineering, pharmacy, and other professional degree programmes.

MHT-CET is one of Maharashtra's most competitive entrance examinations and provides access to top engineering and pharmacy colleges across the state.

The examination evaluates students in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with a syllabus aligned closely with the Maharashtra State Board curriculum.
