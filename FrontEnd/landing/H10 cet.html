The Maharashtra Common Entrance Test (MHT-CET) is a state-level entrance exam conducted for admission to undergraduate courses in Engineering, Pharmacy, and Agriculture in various government and private institutions across Maharashtra.

MHT-CET is organized by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra, and is one of the most competitive exams for students seeking admission to top state colleges like COEP, VJTI, ICT, and others.

The exam primarily tests a student’s understanding of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology based on the State Board (HSC) syllabus, with a focus on speed, accuracy, and time management.
