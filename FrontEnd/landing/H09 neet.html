What is NEET?

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) is India's largest medical entrance examination conducted for admission to MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, and other medical courses across the country.

NEET is the single national-level examination for students aspiring to build careers in medicine and healthcare. It is accepted by government and private medical colleges throughout India.

The examination tests candidates in Biology, Physics, and Chemistry, requiring conceptual clarity, accuracy, and consistent practice.
